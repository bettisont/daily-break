Drag anywhere and release to shoot (pull back like a slingshot). Hit each table's target score before your shots run out, then spend coins on relics. A shot scores chips × mult: chips = ball number × 10; +1 mult per extra ball in the same shot, per ball banked off a cushion and per bomb blast. Ring colours: gold = coins, pink = ×2 mult, red = bomb. Potting the cue ball zeroes the shot. Every third table is a boss.
